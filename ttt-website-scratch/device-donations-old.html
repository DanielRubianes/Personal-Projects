Through the Trees refurbishes, distributes, and resells donated electronics. The proceeds help provide internet access and technology to those in need in our community.
Here are some of the services we provide:

  • Digital Literacy
    Free one-on-one digital literacy sessions are available by appointment at the Tech Spa; We are here to help you feel connected, knowledgeable, and supported in the digital world.
  • Refurbished computers, tablets, cell phones and more at affordable prices
  • Assistance for low-income households:
    • Free devices (cell phones, tablets, and laptops)
    • Subsidised and affordable internet access support
  • Responsible recycling of old and broken electronics

We currently accept the following items:

  • Computers
  • Tablets
  • Cell phones
  • Cameras (film & digital)
  • Gaming systems

Please Note:

  • We do not accept printers.
  • We do not accept TVs.
  • We do not accept stereo systems or bare wire speakers
  • We do not accept DVD/VHS players
  • We accept old and broken computers and will recycle them responsibly.

For more information on preparing your donations, please visit this page.

We take your data security seriously. Before distributing any devices, we ensure that all personal data has been thoroughly wiped. For devices that are recycled, all personal data is destroyed before they are sent for recycling.
